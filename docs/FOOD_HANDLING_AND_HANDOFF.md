# Food handling and handoff workflow

FeastForward is a prototype for coordinating surplus-food donations. A successful match is not proof that food is safe to consume; people handling donations remain responsible for following applicable local food-safety requirements.

## Donor checklist
- [ ] Describe the food accurately, including whether it is packaged, prepared, or perishable.
- [ ] Record preparation time and a realistic pickup deadline.
- [ ] Include allergen information when known.
- [ ] Keep food protected from contamination while awaiting collection.
- [ ] Do not post food that has been stored or handled outside applicable safety guidance.

## Volunteer handoff
1. Confirm the donor, recipient, quantity, and pickup window.
2. Check packaging condition and the donor-provided handling details at pickup.
3. Keep hot and cold items within the appropriate safe temperature controls.
4. Record the handoff time and any issue that prevents delivery.
5. Escalate uncertain or unsafe items rather than distributing them.

## Product behavior
The application should preserve a clear status history (posted, matched, picked up, delivered, cancelled) and avoid marking a donation delivered until the handoff is confirmed. Do not store sensitive recipient details in public-facing food listings.
